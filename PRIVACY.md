## Privacy Policy

In short: you sign in with Google or a code sent to your email; your planner syncs through Supabase; selected task activity goes to OpenAI for an automatic review every 10 completed tasks and for suggesting each day's tasks from the previous day's; signed-in accounts cannot turn these AI features off in the app. We may email you a reminder when tasks you planned for today are unfinished, and at most once a week after 7 days away; you can turn either email off in Profile or from a link in the email. Attached file contents and the Focus notes scratchpad are not sent to AI. We do not sell your data or use advertising trackers. The details and your rights are below.

This notice explains how personal data is processed when you use prune, a web and desktop planner. This version describes the website; the Mac application presents its own notice. It follows the transparency structure of Articles 13 and 14 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). It is not a request to consent to all processing, a waiver of your rights, or a guarantee of regulatory compliance.

## 2. What data do we process, and where does it come from?

- Account and authentication data: the email address you provide, or the email address, name and profile picture of the Google account you sign in with, your account identifier, sign-in/session records and authentication tokens. Supabase handles sign-in with an emailed code and with Google. Your email is sent to Supabase to request a one-time sign-in code; entering the code verifies your account, and the website does not save the code in browser storage or planner data. When you choose Continue with Google, the website first asks Supabase's public settings whether Google sign-in is enabled, then redirects you to Google to authenticate. Google shares your identity information (your name, email address and profile picture) with Supabase, which keeps it with your account to establish your session. The website does not receive your Google password and does not use an account password of its own. This website uses the Supabase browser client to keep your session, including refresh and access tokens, in this browser's localStorage. This is not macOS Keychain storage: scripts running on this website's origin can access it. The desktop app uses a separate native credential store.
- Planner data you enter and activity derived from it: task titles, priorities, estimates, planned and completion dates, task status, timer timestamps and recorded focus time, written stop reasons, and completion statistics. The device supplies an IANA timezone for scheduling and local dates. This is not GPS or precise-location collection.
- Sign-in error reports: when the website cannot reach sign-in services, it sends a short report to this website's own domain naming the step that failed (for example sending a code) and whether the request timed out, failed or the browser was offline. The website host adds your approximate country (from your IP address), browser family and whether you are on a phone or computer, then stores the report in Supabase. Your IP address, full browser details, email address and account are not stored with the report.
- Resource metadata: labels, filenames, URLs and absolute local file paths you attach to tasks. This metadata syncs to Supabase and may contain personal or confidential information. Attached file contents are not uploaded by prune, and prune does not scan your other files. The current website does not display or edit resources; existing metadata is still included in synced task data and the local cache. Add or open resources in the desktop app. The website cannot open absolute Mac file paths.
- Local app data: an account/project-specific unencrypted planner snapshot and pending-action queue in browser localStorage, plus the combined terms/privacy version and acceptance time. The queue and server sync receipts can contain task text, reasons, resource metadata and mutation results. Sign-out does not erase the planner cache or pending actions.
- AI review data: automatic AI reviews are on for every new planner account and cannot be turned off in the app. Accounts that turned them off under an earlier version of this notice stay off until they are turned on in Overview. Each time you complete 10 more tasks, a review is due: the titles, identifiers, dates, status, estimates and recorded time of those 10 tasks, and the identifiers and written reasons of stops recorded on them, are sent to OpenAI. A record of which tasks a review covered is kept so each task is reviewed once. Saved reports include the generated reflection and a source-activity snapshot. Resource links, paths, filenames and file contents are excluded from AI input; sensitive information typed directly into a title or reason is not automatically removed.
- Task suggestion data: while AI reviews are on, opening Today or Tomorrow with open slots asks for suggestions based on the previous day's tasks (yesterday's for Today, today's for Tomorrow). The titles, status, estimates and recorded task time of the previous day's tasks, plus the titles of tasks already planned for the day being planned, are sent to OpenAI. Stop reasons, task identifiers, resource metadata and the Focus notes scratchpad are not sent. Nothing is sent when the previous day had no tasks or the day being planned is already full. Suggestions are returned to your browser and are not saved; a per-account daily attempt counter is kept to limit usage.
- Reminder email data: unless you turn them off, prune sends two kinds of email to your account address. A daily reminder is sent in the morning (about 9:00 to 12:00 in your last reported timezone) only on days when tasks you planned for that day are unfinished; it contains the titles of those tasks. A come-back email is sent at most once a week after you have not used prune for 7 days, and stops after four in a row; it contains no task content. To do this we store, with your account, your last reported IANA timezone, the last time (rounded to within an hour) a signed-in client contacted the planner, your two email choices, the day of your last daily reminder, the time and count of come-back emails, and a random unsubscribe token. The emails contain no tracking pixels added by prune; links open this website. Sensitive text typed into task titles is included in the daily reminder and is visible in your mailbox.
- Support data: your email address, message and any attachments or diagnostics you choose to send when contacting us. Do not send sensitive task content unless necessary to resolve your request.
- Operational metadata: hosting and authentication providers may process IP addresses, request timestamps, technical request/device metadata and security logs. These are distinct from planner content.

We receive data from you, your interactions with prune, your device's selected timezone/file metadata, and our authentication and infrastructure providers. We do not obtain planner data from data brokers. The Focus notes scratchpad stays in the current view's memory; prune does not save, sync or send it to AI, and it is lost when that task/view state is discarded or the app is reloaded.

Avoid entering special-category data (for example health, political or religious information), criminal-offence information, secrets, or other people's personal data into tasks or stop reasons. Automatic AI reviews are not permission to process every possible category of sensitive information. Only attach or enter information you are entitled to use.

## 4. Automatic AI reviews, task suggestions and your choices

AI reviews are part of every planner account: they are enabled automatically for new accounts, no separate AI enable step is required, and the app does not offer a way to turn them off. Accounts that turned reviews off under an earlier version of this notice keep that preference until they choose “Turn on AI reviews” in Overview; once on, reviews stay on. The server checks your account setting before allowing generation and refuses requests to turn reviews off. The backend retains legacy consent-version fields for compatibility; these are not evidence of affirmative consent under this always-on flow.

Reviews no longer run on a weekly schedule. A review becomes due each time you complete 10 tasks that no earlier review covered, counting from the week of 9 November 2026 (earlier work was covered by weekly reviews), and is generated while a signed-in client is open, or at the next launch. It covers those 10 tasks, from the first to the last of their completion dates, and the stops recorded on them. No request is made until 10 tasks are completed. Reviews saved under earlier versions of this notice stay as they are. The website does not offer manual previews.

There is no setting in the app to stop future reviews. You can object to this processing on grounds relating to your particular situation (GDPR Article 21) through the privacy contact channel once one is available (see section 8); we must then stop reviews and suggestions for your account unless we demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms. You can also delete your account in Profile, which disables AI reviews immediately. Stopping reviews does not recall data already transmitted to OpenAI or erase previous reviews automatically. Requests already sent and provider security/retention obligations may continue to apply. You can request erasure of existing reviews and source snapshots through a privacy contact channel once one is available. Tasks, timers and cloud sync continue to work for accounts with reviews off.

Task suggestions follow the same setting: while AI reviews are on, the Today and Tomorrow views may request up to three proposals derived from the previous day's tasks, at most five times per account for each planned day. Tomorrow's proposals are requested again when today's tasks change. The server loads the tasks itself and checks your setting before contacting OpenAI. Suggestions cannot be turned off separately; an objection to AI reviews also stops them. Suggestions are never added to your plan automatically; a task is created only when you choose one.

The OpenAI API is called with store: false. This is not a promise of zero retention, no international transfers, or a particular provider training policy. The operator must verify the applicable API agreement, project controls and provider retention before enabling hosted AI.

AI reflections and suggestions can be inaccurate. They do not score task value, edit tasks or timers, or make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects on you. You decide whether to act on a suggestion.

## 7. How long is data kept?

- Cloud account and planner history, stop reasons, resource metadata, saved AI reviews and their source snapshots: while you maintain your account, until account deletion or a justified earlier administrative deletion. No automatic inactive-account expiry is currently configured. You may request erasure at any time, subject to the limited legal exceptions below. Only the latest 52 saved reviews are displayed, but older reports may remain in the database.
- Server sync receipts: kept with the account until deletion to prevent duplicate replay of pending actions, and may contain earlier request data and results. Removing a resource or changing text in the current planner does not necessarily remove older values from these receipts. We must assess and action an erasure request across these copies, not only the currently visible row.
- Local planner snapshot and pending actions: persist on each device until its browser/site storage is cleared. Sign-out retains them. Clearing local storage can permanently lose unsynced work. Account deletion does not remotely wipe every device's cache; arrange removal of local copies on all your devices. Closing a tab or signing out does not clear the planner cache. Use a trusted browser profile and consider local copies on every device.
- Local terms acceptance and privacy acknowledgement: the current document version and acceptance time remain in this browser profile's localStorage until replaced by a later acceptance or app storage is cleared. This is a device-local record, not an account-wide acceptance or AI-consent register. Another browser or cleared site storage may require acceptance again.
- Session credentials: the website clears its locally saved session on sign-out. Server revocation is best-effort during network failures; an issued access token can remain valid until expiry. The Mac app's session is separate and signing out here does not intentionally sign it out.
- Operational AI content: successful operational retry output is cached for seven days; temporary source activity is cleared on completion/failure or by cleanup after an expired lease. Per-account request/quota metadata is removed after 90 days. Per-account task suggestion attempt counters (date and count only, no task content) are removed after about a week. Sign-in error reports are removed after 90 days. Reminder email settings and send records are kept with the account until it is deleted; copies of sent emails remain in your mailbox and, for a provider-defined period, in the email provider's logs. Aggregate daily usage counters are removed after 90 days and monthly counters after one year. These periods require the retention cleanup job to run successfully and do not delete durable saved reviews.
- Deletion from the active database does not immediately erase provider backups. Restoring a backup must not permanently undo an erasure request.
- OpenAI processing and security retention: store: false does not override provider retention requirements.

We may retain only the data necessary for a specific legal obligation or legal claim for the applicable period, restricting its use accordingly. Data that is genuinely anonymised and no longer reasonably identifies anyone may be retained separately. Pseudonymous identifiers alone do not make data anonymous.

## 9. Security and device privacy

The app uses HTTPS for production cloud connections, Supabase-managed browser sessions, and per-account database access controls. These measures reduce risk but do not make any service completely secure. Browser session storage and the planner cache/outbox are unencrypted site data, not protected by the Mac credential store, and may be accessible to someone with access to your device or backups. Use a protected device account and disk encryption; do not use a shared device for confidential planning without considering these risks.

Our application code is designed not to log credentials or raw AI activity in error messages. Infrastructure logging, administrator access, backup controls and incident response must also be configured appropriately by the operator. If a personal-data breach occurs, the operator must assess and meet any applicable authority and affected-person notification duties.
