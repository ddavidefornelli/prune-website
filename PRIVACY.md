## Privacy Policy

In short: start-up questions stay in your browser until you sign in; your planner syncs through Supabase; selected task activity goes to OpenAI for automatic weekly reviews and for suggesting each day's tasks from the previous day's, unless you turn AI features off in Profile. Attached file contents and the Focus notes scratchpad are not sent to AI. We do not sell your data or use advertising trackers. The details and your rights are below.

This notice explains how personal data is processed when you use prune, a web and desktop planner. This version describes the website; the Mac application presents its own notice. It follows the transparency structure of Articles 13 and 14 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). It is not a request to consent to all processing, a waiver of your rights, or a guarantee of regulatory compliance.

## 2. What data do we process, and where does it come from?

- Guest planner data: without signing in, tasks, recorded focus time and stop reasons stay in an unencrypted journal in this browser's localStorage. No guest planner content is sent to Supabase or OpenAI. Guest use does add to anonymous daily totals kept in Supabase: how many devices started or returned to the guest planner that day, and how many tasks were created, started and completed, with their total recorded focus minutes. To tell new and returning use apart, the app checks this browser's own guest journal; it stores no extra identifier for this. When this browser is shown the start-up questions described below, the same totals also count, separately for each version of the start screen, whether it saw, started or finished them, saw its plan, chose Continue with Google, and later signed in. Each event counts once per browser. The totals contain no task text, stop reasons, survey answers, account or device identifiers, and are not stored per device. Supabase processes the request's IP address and technical metadata as operational metadata, but it is not saved with the totals. Anyone with access to this browser profile may access it. Clearing site data can permanently erase it. Choosing Save & sync transfers this journal's task history and stop reasons to your verified account. The server keeps an import receipt containing the import payload to prevent duplicate transfers; it follows the account's deletion lifecycle. The guest copy is removed only after the server confirms the complete import. Production page-view analytics still apply as described below.
- Start-up questions: a first visit may show a few questions before the planner: what to call you (a first name), what you are here to do, your role, when and for how long you focus, what derails you most, a daily deep-work target, and one outcome you want this week. When they are shown, the planner opens only after you have answered them and signed in with Google; the first name and the week outcome can be left blank. Before you sign in, your answers stay only in this browser's localStorage under prune:survey:v1, together with which version of the start screen this browser was assigned and which counted events it has already sent; nothing you answer is sent to Supabase, OpenAI or our server. The first name never leaves this browser. If you then sign in, the other answers and a week outcome for the current week are transferred with the guest import described above, stored with your account and kept in its import receipt. The answers choose your first three tasks and the default estimate for new tasks on this device; the week outcome is used by that week's automatic review (see below).
- Account and authentication data: the email you provide, your account identifier, sign-in/session records and authentication tokens. Supabase handles email magic-link/code authentication and Google sign-in. If you choose Continue with Google, the website first asks Supabase's public settings whether Google sign-in is enabled, then redirects you to Google to authenticate. Google shares your identity information (your name, email address and profile picture) with Supabase, which keeps it with your account to establish your session. The website does not receive your Google password. Your email is sent to Supabase to request a one-time sign-in link or code; clicking the link or entering a code verifies your account. The website does not use an account password and does not save the code in browser storage or planner data. This website uses the Supabase browser client to keep your session, including refresh and access tokens, in this browser's localStorage. This is not macOS Keychain storage: scripts running on this website's origin can access it. The desktop app uses a separate native credential store.
- Planner data you enter and activity derived from it: task titles, priorities, estimates, planned and completion dates, task status, timer timestamps and recorded focus time, written stop reasons, and completion statistics. The device supplies an IANA timezone for scheduling and local dates. This is not GPS or precise-location collection.
- Resource metadata: labels, filenames, URLs and absolute local file paths you attach to tasks. This metadata syncs to Supabase and may contain personal or confidential information. Attached file contents are not uploaded by prune, and prune does not scan your other files. The current website does not display or edit resources; existing metadata is still included in synced task data and the local cache. Add or open resources in the desktop app. The website cannot open absolute Mac file paths.
- Local app data: an account/project-specific unencrypted planner snapshot and pending-action queue in browser localStorage, plus the combined terms/privacy version and acceptance time. The queue and server sync receipts can contain task text, reasons, resource metadata and mutation results. Sign-out does not erase the planner cache or pending actions.
- Weekly AI data: automatic weekly reviews are on for newly created planner accounts unless you turn them off in Profile. When a review is due, selected task titles, task identifiers, dates, status, estimates, recorded task time, stop identifiers and written stop reasons are sent to OpenAI, together with the week outcome you wrote in the start-up questions if it was for that week. Saved weekly reports include the generated reflection and a source-activity snapshot. Resource links, paths, filenames and file contents are excluded from AI input; sensitive information typed directly into a title or reason is not automatically removed.
- Task suggestion data: while weekly AI is on, opening Today or Tomorrow with open slots asks for suggestions based on the previous day's tasks (yesterday's for Today, today's for Tomorrow). The titles, status, estimates and recorded task time of the previous day's tasks, plus the titles of tasks already planned for the day being planned, are sent to OpenAI. Stop reasons, task identifiers, resource metadata and the Focus notes scratchpad are not sent. Nothing is sent when the previous day had no tasks or the day being planned is already full. Suggestions are returned to your browser and are not saved; a per-account daily attempt counter is kept to limit usage.
- Support data: your email address, message and any attachments or diagnostics you choose to send when contacting us. Do not send sensitive task content unless necessary to resolve your request.
- Operational metadata: hosting, authentication and email providers may process IP addresses, request timestamps, technical request/device metadata, delivery records and security logs. These are distinct from planner content.

We receive data from you, your interactions with prune, your device's selected timezone/file metadata, and our authentication and infrastructure providers. We do not obtain planner data from data brokers. The Focus notes scratchpad stays in the current view's memory; prune does not save, sync or send it to AI, and it is lost when that task/view state is discarded or the app is reloaded.

Avoid entering special-category data (for example health, political or religious information), criminal-offence information, secrets, or other people's personal data into tasks or stop reasons. Automatic weekly reviews are not permission to process every possible category of sensitive information. Only attach or enter information you are entitled to use.

## 4. Automatic weekly AI reviews, task suggestions and your choices

Weekly reviews are enabled automatically for new planner accounts; no separate AI enable step is required. Existing account preferences are preserved, including disabled reviews. The server checks your account setting before allowing generation. The backend retains legacy consent-version fields for compatibility; these are not evidence of affirmative consent under this default-on flow.

Reviews run Sunday at 4:00 p.m. in your device's local timezone while a signed-in client is open, or catch up at the next launch. They cover Monday through generation time; later Sunday activity is not included. If several weeks were missed, only the latest due week is reviewed. No request is made for a week without recorded tasks or stops. If you wrote a week outcome in the start-up questions, that week's review receives it as context and may mention which activity relates to it; it does not score you against it. The website does not offer manual previews.

Open Profile and choose “Turn off weekly reviews” to stop future reviews at any time. Turning reviews off prevents new generation reservations and prevents in-flight results being saved if the preference revision has changed. It does not recall data already transmitted to OpenAI or erase previous reviews automatically. Requests already sent and provider security/retention obligations may continue to apply. You can turn reviews back on in Profile, or request erasure of existing reviews and source snapshots through a privacy contact channel once one is available. Tasks, timers and cloud sync continue to work with reviews off.

Task suggestions follow the same setting: while weekly reviews are on, the Today and Tomorrow views may request up to three proposals derived from the previous day's tasks, at most five times per account for each planned day. Tomorrow's proposals are requested again when today's tasks change. The server loads the tasks itself and checks your setting before contacting OpenAI. Turning weekly reviews off in Profile also stops suggestions. Suggestions are never added to your plan automatically; a task is created only when you choose one.

The OpenAI API is called with store: false. This is not a promise of zero retention, no international transfers, or a particular provider training policy. The operator must verify the applicable API agreement, project controls and provider retention before enabling hosted AI.

AI reflections and suggestions can be inaccurate. They do not score task value, edit tasks or timers, or make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects on you. You decide whether to act on a suggestion.

## 7. How long is data kept?

- Cloud account and planner history, stop reasons, resource metadata, saved weekly reviews and their source snapshots: while you maintain your account, until account deletion or a justified earlier administrative deletion. No automatic inactive-account expiry is currently configured. You may request erasure at any time, subject to the limited legal exceptions below. Only the latest 52 saved reviews are displayed, but older reports may remain in the database.
- Start-up answers: in this browser until you clear site data, including after sign-in, where they keep personalizing this device. Answers transferred at sign-in (never the first name) are kept with the account until deletion.
- Server sync receipts: kept with the account until deletion to prevent duplicate replay of pending actions, and may contain earlier request data and results. Removing a resource or changing text in the current planner does not necessarily remove older values from these receipts. We must assess and action an erasure request across these copies, not only the currently visible row.
- Local planner snapshot and pending actions: persist on each device until its browser/site storage is cleared. Sign-out retains them. Clearing local storage can permanently lose unsynced work. Account deletion does not remotely wipe every device's cache; arrange removal of local copies on all your devices. Closing a tab or signing out does not clear the planner cache. Use a trusted browser profile and consider local copies on every device.
- Local terms acceptance and privacy acknowledgement: the current document version and acceptance time remain in this browser profile's localStorage until replaced by a later acceptance or app storage is cleared. This is a device-local record, not an account-wide acceptance or AI-consent register. Another browser or cleared site storage may require acceptance again.
- Session credentials: the website clears its locally saved session on sign-out. Server revocation is best-effort during network failures; an issued access token can remain valid until expiry. The Mac app's session is separate and signing out here does not intentionally sign it out.
- Operational AI content: successful operational retry output is cached for seven days; temporary source activity is cleared on completion/failure or by cleanup after an expired lease. Per-account request/quota metadata is removed after 90 days. Per-account task suggestion attempt counters (date and count only, no task content) are removed after about a week. Aggregate daily usage counters are removed after 90 days and monthly counters after one year. These periods require the retention cleanup job to run successfully and do not delete durable saved reviews.
- Anonymous guest usage totals: kept until the operator deletes them. They are not linked to a device or account, so they cannot be retrieved or erased for an individual user.
- Deletion from the active database does not immediately erase provider backups. Restoring a backup must not permanently undo an erasure request.
- OpenAI processing and security retention: store: false does not override provider retention requirements.

We may retain only the data necessary for a specific legal obligation or legal claim for the applicable period, restricting its use accordingly. Data that is genuinely anonymised and no longer reasonably identifies anyone may be retained separately. Pseudonymous identifiers alone do not make data anonymous.

## 9. Security and device privacy

The app uses HTTPS for production cloud connections, Supabase-managed browser sessions, and per-account database access controls. These measures reduce risk but do not make any service completely secure. Browser session storage and the planner cache/outbox are unencrypted site data, not protected by the Mac credential store, and may be accessible to someone with access to your device or backups. Use a protected device account and disk encryption; do not use a shared device for confidential planning without considering these risks.

Our application code is designed not to log credentials or raw AI activity in error messages. Infrastructure logging, administrator access, backup controls and incident response must also be configured appropriately by the operator. If a personal-data breach occurs, the operator must assess and meet any applicable authority and affected-person notification duties.
